Frequently asked questions

What teams ask before they put Needl.ai to work.

What is Needl.ai?

Needl.ai is private enterprise AI for financial markets. Private equity and private credit teams use it for AI due diligence, from the data room to a cited investment committee memo. It reads your data rooms, filings, research, email and drives, and answers the questions your investment, credit and research teams ask every day, with a citation behind every number.

Where does Needl.ai run, and who can see our data?

Needl.ai runs in your own cloud account, on premise, or in a dedicated instance we operate. It follows the permissions of each source system, so people only see what they can already open, and it never trains on your data. More on Trust & Security.

What can teams do with Needl.ai?

Read a data room and draft the investment committee memo, draft credit rating reports in your house format, write coverage notes, watch a portfolio or the regulators you cover, and answer questions across your own research, each with a source.

Which systems does Needl.ai connect to?

Your email, files, chat, CRM and data warehouses, plus public filings and news. See the full list on Integrations.
