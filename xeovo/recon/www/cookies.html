> Cookies Policy

We use cookies only to help you log in and keep your account secure. We do not use analytics or tracking cookies.

  • csrftoken (expires after one year)
    is used to prevent Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) attacks.
  • referrer (expires after one year)
    is created when someone invites you through referral link.
  • sessionid (expires after 2 days)
    is used to identify a user that has logged into a website.
  • __stripe_sid (expires after 30 minutes)
    is implemented by Stripe and created when you proceed to the Stripe payment page.
  • __stripe_mid (expires after one year)
    is implemented by Stripe and created when you proceed to the Stripe payment page.
