Source: HuntBug listing (https://huntbug.com/open-directory) + Survicate program terms (from search results).

## Program Overview
- Company: Survicate S.A.
- Platform: HuntBug (listed in HuntBug open directory)
- Status: Live/Public (appears in HuntBug open directory)
- Focus: Survey/feedback platform

- JavaScript SDK

- Data Export API v2 only
- Webhooks

## Out of Scope
From §4 and §4A of program terms:
- Any domains, endpoints, assets, applications, or environments not listed in the Targets section are out of scope
- Vulnerabilities in third-party applications or libraries that are not developed by Survicate
- Circumventing business plan limits defined in Survicate’s pricing and using features without paying for them, including paywall bypass attacks
- Out-of-scope and low-quality submissions are not eligible for any Reward regardless of theoretical severity
- Submissions may be rejected on preliminary basis as out of scope/low quality
